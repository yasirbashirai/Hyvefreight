Who We Are

We're technically freight brokers.
We operate like freight partners.

Hyve Freight Services was built on a simple belief: moving freight is a relationship business. Anyone can post a load. A partner learns your business, your lanes, your customers and your pressure points, then executes like their name is on the truck.

From our home base in Litchfield Park, Arizona, we connect shippers with vetted, reliable capacity across the country, backed by 24/7 communication and a team that answers the phone when it matters.

  • Relationships and communication first, no dispatch black holes
  • Deep freight expertise across equipment types and markets
  • Flexible transportation solutions shaped around your business
  • Disciplined execution from pickup through final delivery
