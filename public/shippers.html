Capabilities

Every mode. One standard.

Whatever the freight, it runs on the HEX Standard: expertise up front, execution in transit, excellence at delivery.

Full Truckload (FTL)

Dedicated dry van capacity for full loads, coast to coast. Competitive market pricing, vetted carriers, and proactive check calls from pickup to POD, your freight never travels unattended.

Less-Than-Truckload (LTL)

Partial shipments consolidated intelligently, protecting your freight, your transit times and your cost per pallet. Ideal for recurring regional distribution.

Refrigerated (Reefer)

Temperature-controlled trucking for food, beverage and sensitive product. Continuous temp monitoring, clean documentation, and carriers who treat cold chain like a discipline.

Flatbed & Open Deck

Building materials, steel, machinery and oversized freight, moved by carriers who know securement, tarping, permits and job-site delivery windows.

Expedited / Time-Critical

When the clock is the commodity: team drivers, priority dispatch and constant communication until it's signed for. Plant shutdowns, launch dates, recovery loads.

Specialized Solutions

Unusual freight, unusual lanes, unusual timelines. Tell us the constraint and we'll engineer the transportation solution around it, that's the partner part.
