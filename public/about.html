About Hyve

The name says brokers.
The work says partners.

Hyve Freight Services exists because freight deserves better than load boards and voicemail. It deserves a partner.

Our Story

Built in Arizona.
Invested in your success.

Hyve Freight Services is a freight brokerage headquartered in Litchfield Park, Arizona. On paper, that makes us brokers. In practice, we operate like an extension of your team — learning your business, protecting your customers and treating every load like our name is on the trailer.

The hexagon in our logo isn't decoration. Like a hive, freight works when every side connects — shipper, carrier, receiver, broker. Our job is making every one of those connections stronger, so every delivery is seamless.
